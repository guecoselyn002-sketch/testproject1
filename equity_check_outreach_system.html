Tool stack + justification

Apollo.io

RIA/wealth manager database. Filter by AUM, firm type, location, title. Exports to CSV for enrichment.

LinkedIn Sales Nav

Behavioral signals — who posted, who engaged, who changed roles in last 90 days. Warm intent layer.

Clay

Enrichment automation. Pulls LinkedIn activity, company news, recent posts — feeds AI personalization row by row.

Claude / GPT-4o

Drafts the personalized first lines and subject lines. Never the final word — always edited for founder voice.

Instantly / Lemlist

Sequenced email delivery with warmup, tracking, reply detection. Stops sequence on reply automatically.

Attio CRM

Pipeline tracking. Every prospect has a stage, last action, next follow-up, and signal notes. No leads fall through.

Notion

Master playbook, prompt library, sequence templates, and weekly review dashboard. Single source of truth.

Calendly

One-click booking link in final CTA. Removes friction at the moment of interest. Routes to founder calendar.

Prospect research workflow

1

Build the target list in Apollo

Filter: Title = "Wealth Manager" OR "RIA" OR "Financial Advisor" / AUM = $100M–$2B / Geography = US / Firm type = Independent RIA or Wirehouse. Export 200 contacts per batch. Remove anyone with no LinkedIn activity in 90 days.

2

Enrich each contact in Clay

Clay pulls: last 3 LinkedIn posts, recent company news, any mutual connections, firm's recent performance filings. Each row now has a "personalization data" column that feeds directly into AI prompts.

3

Score each prospect (1–3)

Score 3: Posted about private markets, alternatives, or client portfolio diversification in last 30 days. Score 2: Active on LinkedIn, works at firm with $250M+ AUM. Score 1: Cold — no activity signal. Only Score 3 and 2 go into the outreach pipeline.

4

Build a 1-paragraph prospect brief

Run AI prompt #1 (see prompts tab) with the enriched data. Output: a 3-sentence brief covering their current positioning, what they likely care about, and the angle most likely to resonate. This is the foundation of every message.

5

Write the personalized first line

Run AI prompt #2. This generates the opening sentence for the outreach — specific enough to prove you did research, warm enough to not feel automated. Human reviews and approves before sending. This is the quality gate.

6

Load into Instantly and tag in Attio

Approved contacts enter the email sequence in Instantly. Simultaneously created in Attio at Stage 1 (Identified) with all research notes attached. Sequence begins within 24 hours of tagging.

AI prompts — copy-paste ready

Prompt 1 Prospect brief generator
Input: paste enriched Clay data
You are a senior financial services business development analyst. Based on the following information about a wealth manager prospect, write a 3-sentence prospect brief. Prospect data: [PASTE CLAY ENRICHMENT OUTPUT HERE] Brief must include: 1. Their current positioning (what kind of clients they serve, what they focus on) 2. What they likely care about right now based on their recent activity 3. The single most relevant angle for Equity Check to lead with Rules: No fluff. No generic statements. If the data is thin, say so — do not invent. Output only the 3-sentence brief, nothing else.
Prompt 2 Personalized first line generator
Input: prospect brief from Prompt 1
You are writing the opening line of a cold outreach email to a wealth manager or RIA. This line must feel like it was written by a human who did actual research — not by AI. Prospect brief: [PASTE BRIEF FROM PROMPT 1] Rules: - Reference something specific and real from their brief - Do NOT start with "I" or "I noticed" - Do NOT be complimentary ("Great post on...") - Keep it under 25 words - Tone: direct, peer-to-peer, not salesy - Output: one sentence only, no explanation Examples of good first lines: "Your recent shift toward alternatives for HNW clients caught my attention — specifically the allocation timing question." "The conversation around fee compression and product differentiation in independent RIAs is one we've been thinking about too."
Prompt 3 Follow-up email writer
Input: original message + no reply signal
Write a follow-up email for a wealth manager prospect who has not replied to our first outreach. The tone must shift slightly — less about us, more about them. Context: - Original message: [PASTE EMAIL 1] - Days since sent: [X] - Any new signal from prospect (post, comment, news): [ADD IF ANY] Rules: - Do not restate the original pitch - Add one new piece of value: a stat, a market shift, or a relevant insight - Keep under 80 words - End with a low-friction question, not a CTA to book - No "just checking in" — that phrase is banned
Prompt 4 LinkedIn comment warm-up writer
Input: prospect's recent LinkedIn post
Write a LinkedIn comment on behalf of a capital markets founder responding to a wealth manager's post. The comment must add genuine value to the conversation. Post content: [PASTE THEIR LINKEDIN POST] Rules: - Add a perspective or data point they did not mention - Do NOT pitch, mention Equity Check, or drop a link - Under 3 sentences - Tone: confident, knowledgeable, peer-level - This comment is about building trust before outreach — it must earn a reply or a profile view on its own merits
Prompt 5 Meeting prep brief generator
Input: full prospect profile before a booked call
You are preparing a founder for a 30-minute discovery call with a wealth manager prospect. Generate a call prep brief. Prospect profile: [PASTE FULL ENRICHMENT + CONVERSATION HISTORY] Output format: 1. Who they are (2 sentences max) 2. What they likely want from this call 3. The 2 questions most likely to open a real conversation 4. One thing to avoid saying 5. Suggested 30-second opening that leads with their world, not ours Keep it under 200 words. This is a scan-before-call doc, not an essay.

Full outreach sequence — RIA at $500M AUM firm

Prospect profile

Name: Michael Chen · Title: Managing Partner · Firm: Clearwater Wealth Advisors, Denver CO · AUM: $520M · Signal: Posted last week about "alternative allocations becoming table stakes for HNW clients" · Score: 3 (hot)

Day 1 — Email 1 (LinkedIn warm-up happens 3 days before this) Cold intro

Subject: Alternatives allocation — timing question

Michael,

Your point about alternatives becoming table stakes for HNW clients is one we're seeing play out in how advisors are thinking about private real estate specifically — the allocation timing question keeps coming up.

We're Equity Check. We work with RIAs looking to expand private market access for clients without taking on the due diligence burden themselves. Our model is built on operator-level diligence and alignment structures most managers don't offer.

Worth a 20-minute conversation to see if there's fit?

Andrew Davis
Equity Check

Day 3 — LinkedIn connection request Warm touch

(Connection note, 200 char limit)

Hi Michael — I sent you a note via email this week. Also following your work around alternative allocations for HNW clients. Worth connecting here too.

Day 5 — Email 2 Value add

Subject: Re: Alternatives allocation — timing question

Michael,

One thing I forgot to mention: the advisors we work with are typically allocating between 8–15% of HNW portfolios to private real estate opportunities. The ones who find us most useful aren't looking for more product — they're looking for better diligence and alignment structures.

Is that a challenge your team is running into right now?

Andrew

Day 8 — LinkedIn DM (if connected) Soft nudge

Hey Michael — saw you connected. Appreciate it. Just wanted to make sure my emails didn't get buried. Happy to keep it brief — even a 15-minute intro call would tell us both quickly if there's any fit here.

Day 12 — Email 3 Pattern break

Subject: Last note from me on this

Michael,

I don't want to keep pinging you if this isn't relevant. But before I close the loop — one thing advisors tell us consistently: the gap in private market access isn't product supply, it's confidence in the diligence behind the deal.

If that's a question your team wrestles with, I think we'd have an interesting 20 minutes.

If not — no worries at all. I'll stop here.

Andrew

If they reply — next steps

A

Move to "Engaged" in Attio. Run Prompt 5 to generate call prep brief. Book via Calendly link in reply.

B

If they say "not now" — tag as "Nurture" in Attio. Set 60-day follow-up. Add to LinkedIn content warming list.

Quality vs. quantity — how we measure

Reply rate target

8–12%

Industry avg is 2–4%. Below 6% = rewrite the sequence.

Meeting conversion rate

25–35%

Of all replies, this % should book a call.

Weekly outreach volume

50–80

Score 2+ only. Quality gate before volume.

Weekly meetings target

3–5

Primary success metric. Everything else is a lever.

Weekly review checklist (every Friday)

— How many contacts moved from Stage 1 → 2 this week?
— How many replies received? What % of total sent?
— How many meetings booked vs. target?
— Any Stage 3 contacts that haven't been followed up in 5+ days?
— Which message angle got the most opens/replies? → Update sequence.

— Re-read 5 random sent emails. Do they still sound human?
— Any first lines that feel generic? Rewrite them.
— Any new market signal we should be referencing? (Fed rates, private RE news, RIA consolidation trends)
— Update the "value add" email if market context has shifted.

— Which Nurture contacts have a reactivation date this week?
— Has any Nurture contact posted on LinkedIn recently? → New signal = back to Sequence.
— Any "not now" contacts from 60+ days ago worth re-engaging with a new angle?
